## 🎤 What It Is
Karaoke Software for Windows is free karaoke software for PC that makes hosting a sing-along effortless. It plays your karaoke tracks with lyrics scrolling on screen, keeps a rotation of singers, and lets you tweak the key so every voice sounds its best. Made for house parties and casual practice, it keeps things simple: load your songs, build a queue, and pass the mic. Everyone gets their moment without anyone fussing over settings.

## ⭐ Key Features
> Free karaoke software that keeps the party moving from the first song to the last.

- 🎼 **Synced lyrics** — Show clear, scrolling lyrics so singers always know what comes next.
- 👥 **Singer rotation** — Queue up who sings next and keep the lineup fair and fun.
- 🎚️ **Key control** — Raise or lower the pitch to match anyone's vocal range.
- 🖥️ **Second-screen lyrics** — Push lyrics to a TV while you run the show from your PC.
- 📚 **Playlists** — Organize your songs and search your library instantly.
